# COMPOSITION

## Structure
The Hero section follows a strict, 4-level composition pattern (LESS UI, MORE PRODUCT):
1. **Eyebrow:** `Digital Display & Presentation` (Clean, capitalized, primary color).
2. **Headline:** `Turn every screen into a digital experience.` (Natural wrapping, max 3 lines on desktop, ~64px max).
3. **Supporting Copy & CTAs:** Concise description max 480px width, followed immediately by action buttons and a muted capability proof line (`Offline-first · Multi-screen · Remote control`).
4. **Product Showcase:** Dominates the right side, overlapping the grid bounds slightly. 

## Typography & Scale
- Headline is kept in check (max-w: 600px).
- Line-height optimized to `1.04` for tight, confident reading.
- No redundant brand names injected into the flow.

## Spatial Alignment
- `min-h-[calc(100vh-56px)]` ensures the hero breathes perfectly.
- `lg:items-center` creates optical balance against the large product mass on the right.
