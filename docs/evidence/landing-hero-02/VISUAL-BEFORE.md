# VISUAL BEFORE (HERO-01)

The previous implementation (HERO-01) suffered from a fragmented hierarchy:
1. **Redundant Title:** The word "Vitrine360" broke the flow between the eyebrow and the headline.
2. **Vertical Bloat:** Forced line breaks in the headline created a towering column of text that pushed CTAs down.
3. **Player Weight:** The player was styled as a generic UI card with thick borders and a heavy playlist, reducing its perceived value as a premium SaaS product.
4. **Grid Constraint:** The player was confined to a small bounding box, failing to act as a dominant visual anchor.
